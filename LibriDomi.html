Privacy boundaries

Choosing a local file keeps that book on this device. Remote images, fonts and media embedded in EPUB pages are blocked. Importing a public URL contacts that URL only after you press Import. Opening an external link inside a book requires confirmation.

Offline behavior

The reader itself is one file. EPUB.js and JSZip are pinned CDN dependencies. Once your browser has cached them the page can often reopen offline, but browsers may evict HTTP cache entries.

Device-local storage

Books do not automatically synchronize between browsers or devices. Clearing site data, private-browsing data or browser storage removes the local shelf.
